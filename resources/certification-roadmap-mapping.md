# 📜 Cybersecurity Certification & TryHackMe Free Lab Mapping

This guide maps popular **100% FREE TryHackMe Rooms** directly to major cybersecurity industry certification domains.

## 🛡️ Defensive & Blue Team Certifications

### 1. CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)
- **General Security Concepts:** [Principles of Security](https://tryhackme.com/room/principlesofsecurity)
- **Threats, Attacks & Vulnerabilities:** [OWASP Top 10](https://tryhackme.com/room/owasptop10), [Phishing Emails 101](https://tryhackme.com/room/phishingemails101)
- **Architecture & Design:** [Network Defenses](https://tryhackme.com/room/networkdefenses), [Firewalls](https://tryhackme.com/room/firewalls)
- **Cryptography & PKI:** [Intro to Cryptography](https://tryhackme.com/room/cryptographyintro)

### 2. CompTIA CySA+ (CS0-003) & Blue Team Level 1 (BTL1)
- **Security Operations & Monitoring:** [Intro to SIEM](https://tryhackme.com/room/introtosiem), [Splunk Basics](https://tryhackme.com/room/splunk101)
- **Log Analysis:** [Windows Event Logs](https://tryhackme.com/room/windowsevents), [Sysmon](https://tryhackme.com/room/sysmon)
- **Packet & Traffic Analysis:** [Wireshark Basics](https://tryhackme.com/room/wiresharkthebasics), [Zeek](https://tryhackme.com/room/zeekbro)
- **Incident Response:** [Incident Response Methodology](https://tryhackme.com/room/incidentresponsemethodology), [Investigat3d](https://tryhackme.com/room/investigat3d)

### 3. Microsoft SC-200 (Security Operations Analyst)
- **Mitigate threats using Microsoft Defender & Sentinel:** [Windows Event Logs](https://tryhackme.com/room/windowsevents), [Sysmon](https://tryhackme.com/room/sysmon)
- **Threat Hunting:** [YARA](https://tryhackme.com/room/yara), [Snort](https://tryhackme.com/room/snort)
