A curated list of **free TryHackMe rooms** focused entirely on **Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI)** and **Threat Hunting** training. ## 💼 Recommended Hands-on Portfolio Projects
1. **Threat Actor Profile & TTP Brief:** Select an active threat group (e.g., Volt Typhoon, APT28), map their known TTPs to the MITRE ATT&CK Navigator, and create an intelligence brief.
2. **Threat Hunting Hypothesis Report:** Formulate a hypothesis for hunting encoded PowerShell execution, test it against event logs, and document the findings.
